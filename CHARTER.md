# Open Tagalog Charter

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Ratified
**Ratification Date:** 30 July 2026

---

> **This Charter is the constitutional document of the Open Tagalog Project.**

It establishes the project's enduring mission, vision, principles, governance, and long-term direction.

Unlike ordinary project documentation, this Charter is intended to remain stable over time and should only be amended when there is a compelling and well-documented reason that cannot reasonably be addressed through any other project documentation.

---

# Motto

> **Making Tagalog accessible to everyone, everywhere.**

---

# Mission

To provide the world's most comprehensive, professionally structured, and freely available Tagalog course for English speakers.

Open Tagalog exists to remove financial, geographical, and educational barriers by making high-quality Tagalog learning resources freely available to anyone with an interest in learning the language.

---

# Vision

A world where anyone, anywhere can learn Tagalog through high-quality, freely accessible educational resources.

Open Tagalog aspires to become the global reference for free Tagalog education by combining academic quality, practical communication skills, modern educational design, and open collaboration.

---

# Purpose

Learning a language should not depend on the ability to pay.

Many excellent language-learning resources exist behind paywalls, while freely available resources are often incomplete, inconsistent, or difficult to follow.

Open Tagalog was created to change that.

Our purpose is to build a complete learning ecosystem that enables learners to progress from absolute beginner to confident communicator through professionally structured, openly licensed educational materials.

---

# Core Values

## Accessibility

Education should be available to everyone regardless of location, background, or financial circumstances.

---

## Openness

Knowledge grows through collaboration.

Open Tagalog embraces open-source principles and Open Educational Resources (OER), encouraging responsible contributions that improve the project for everyone.

---

## Quality

Educational quality always takes precedence over speed.

Accuracy, clarity, consistency, and maintainability are fundamental to every part of the project.

---

## Practicality

Teach the language people actually use.

Real communication should always take precedence over artificial textbook examples whenever educationally appropriate.

---

## Respect

Language is inseparable from culture.

Open Tagalog seeks to present the Filipino language and its cultures with respect, curiosity, accuracy, and humility.

---

## Sustainability

Every decision should support the long-term health of the project.

Open Tagalog is intended to remain useful, maintainable, and freely available for decades.

---

# Educational Philosophy

Open Tagalog emphasizes understanding rather than memorization.

Learners should understand how the language works rather than simply memorize vocabulary lists or grammar rules.

Lessons should encourage active learning through reading, listening, speaking, writing, observation, and continuous review.

Whenever practical, explain **why** something works instead of merely stating **what** is correct.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **When in doubt, optimize for the learner.**

This principle guides every educational, editorial, organizational, and technical decision within the Open Tagalog Project.

If several correct approaches exist, contributors should choose the one that best helps learners understand while remaining accurate.

Every contribution should answer one simple question:

> **Does this make learning easier?**

---

# Longevity Principle

> **Every major decision should still make sense ten years from now.**

Open Tagalog is intended to become a lasting educational resource rather than a short-term project.

Contributors should favor solutions that are:

* Open rather than proprietary.
* Well documented rather than dependent upon individuals.
* Maintainable rather than unnecessarily complex.
* Stable rather than fashionable.
* Portable rather than platform-dependent.
* Sustainable rather than merely convenient.

The project should avoid unnecessary dependence on commercial services, proprietary technologies, or single vendors whenever practical.

Success should be measured not only by what the project accomplishes today, but also by how well it continues serving learners many years into the future.

---

# Scope

Open Tagalog aims to provide a complete educational ecosystem, including but not limited to:

* A structured curriculum from A0 to B2.
* Professional textbook series.
* Printable student workbooks.
* Interactive digital workbooks.
* Grammar reference.
* Vocabulary dictionary.
* Pronunciation guide.
* Listening resources.
* Speaking practice.
* Assessments and examinations.
* Teacher resources.
* A modern educational website.
* Downloadable learning materials.
* Open educational data.

---

# Non-Goals

To preserve a clear and sustainable direction, Open Tagalog explicitly defines what it is **not** intended to become.

## Not a Commercial Product

The project's purpose is education, not profit maximization.

---

## Not a Replacement for Teachers

Open Tagalog complements teachers, schools, tutors, and classrooms.

It does not seek to replace them.

---

## Not a Social Media Platform

Community interaction should always support learning rather than become the project's primary purpose.

---

## No Artificial Restrictions

Educational content should never be intentionally restricted to encourage paid upgrades or subscriptions.

---

## No Vendor Lock-In

Learners should never be required to use proprietary software or a particular platform to access educational materials whenever practical alternatives exist.

---

## No Mandatory User Accounts

Access to the educational content should never require registration.

Optional accounts may exist in the future for convenience features, but not for basic learning.

---

## No Unnecessary Data Collection

Privacy should be respected by design.

Collect only the information genuinely necessary to operate optional services.

---

## No Artificial Intelligence Dependency

Artificial intelligence may assist in creating, reviewing, or improving educational materials.

However, the project itself must remain understandable, maintainable, and editable by humans without requiring proprietary AI systems.

---

## No Sacrifice of Quality for Speed

Publishing quickly must never take priority over educational quality.

---

## No Mission Drift

Every new feature, partnership, technology, or proposal should strengthen—not dilute—the mission of Open Tagalog.

---

# Open Educational Resource Commitment

Open Tagalog is committed to remaining an Open Educational Resource.

Educational materials should remain freely available for learning, teaching, translation, adaptation, and redistribution under open licenses.

Knowledge should become more accessible as the project grows, not less.

---

# Governance

Open Tagalog values transparency.

Major educational, technical, organizational, and architectural decisions should be documented.

Respectful discussion is encouraged.

Constructive disagreement is welcomed.

Decisions should be based upon evidence, educational benefit, and long-term sustainability.

---

# Community

Open Tagalog welcomes learners, educators, native speakers, linguists, developers, designers, translators, researchers, and volunteers.

Every constructive contribution strengthens the project.

The success of Open Tagalog depends upon collaboration, mutual respect, and a shared commitment to improving access to education.

---

# Decision Framework

Before implementing any significant change, contributors should ask:

* Does this improve learning?
* Does this align with our mission?
* Does this follow our educational standards?
* Does this preserve openness?
* Does this support long-term sustainability?
* Will this decision still make sense ten years from now?

If the answer is "yes" to these questions, the proposal is likely aligned with the Charter.

---

# Charter Stability

This Charter is intended to remain stable over time.

Changes should be considered extraordinary.

Before proposing an amendment, contributors should first ask:

* Can this objective be achieved without modifying the Charter?
* Can the issue be addressed through supporting documentation instead?
* Would this amendment change the long-term identity of Open Tagalog?

If the answer suggests that modification is unnecessary, the Charter should remain unchanged.

Stability is a feature—not a limitation.

---

# Commitment

Open Tagalog is founded upon one simple belief:

> **High-quality education should be freely available to everyone.**

Every lesson, every explanation, every exercise, every correction, every contribution, and every improvement should move the project closer to that goal.

---

# Ratification

This Charter was ratified by the founders of the Open Tagalog Project on **30 July 2026**.

It establishes the enduring principles upon which Open Tagalog is built.

Future contributors are encouraged to preserve both the letter and the spirit of this Charter.

The Charter should evolve only when there is a compelling, well-documented reason that cannot reasonably be addressed through any other project documentation.

Open Tagalog is more than a collection of lessons.

It is a long-term commitment to making Tagalog learning professionally structured, openly licensed, freely accessible, and continuously improving for learners around the world.
