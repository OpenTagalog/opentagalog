# Basic Greetings in Tagalog

Welcome to your first Tagalog lesson! These are some of the most common phrases used when meeting people.

## Hello

<VocabularyCard
  tagalog="Kumusta"
  pronunciation="koo-moos-TAH"
  english="Hello / How are you?"
/>

<AudioButton text="Kumusta" />

Example:

> Kumusta ka?

**Meaning:** How are you?

---

## Good morning

**Tagalog:** Magandang umaga

**Pronunciation:** ma-GAN-dang oo-MA-ga

**Meaning:** Good morning

Example:

> Magandang umaga po.

**Meaning:** Good morning (polite)

The word **po** is added to show respect, especially when speaking with elders or people you do not know well.

---

## Good afternoon

**Tagalog:** Magandang hapon

**Pronunciation:** ma-GAN-dang ha-PON

**Meaning:** Good afternoon

---

## Good evening

**Tagalog:** Magandang gabi

**Pronunciation:** ma-GAN-dang ga-BEE

**Meaning:** Good evening / Good night

---

## Thank you

**Tagalog:** Salamat

**Pronunciation:** sa-LA-mat

**Meaning:** Thank you

Example:

> Salamat po sa iyong kabaitan.

**Meaning:** Thank you for your kindness.

---

## You are welcome

**Tagalog:** Walang anuman

**Pronunciation:** wa-LANG a-noo-MAN

**Meaning:** You are welcome

---

## Goodbye

**Tagalog:** Paalam

**Pronunciation:** pa-a-LAM

**Meaning:** Goodbye

A more common everyday expression:

> Ingat!

**Meaning:** Take care!

---

## Useful first phrases

| English | Tagalog |
|---|---|
| I like it | Gusto ko ito |
| I don't like it | Ayoko nito |
| Please | Pakiusap |
| Excuse me | Makikiraan po |
| Sorry | Pasensya na |
| Yes | Oo |
| No | Hindi |
